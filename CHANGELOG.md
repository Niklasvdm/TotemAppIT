# Changelog

Dated, semver-tagged history for Totem Finder (IT). The single source of truth for the current build is [`VERSION`](VERSION); the live [Issues & Roadmap](README.md#issues--roadmap) stays in the [README](README.md). Tags: `[FEATURE]` new capability, `[CHANGE]` behaviour change, `[BUG]`/`[FIX]` fix, `[ENHANCEMENT]` polish, `[DOCS]`, `[CHORE]`, `[SECURITY]`, `[TEST]`.

### 0.5.0 — 2026-10-12
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: enemies, bosses and sheets aligned to the refactored design note.** Robot archetypes are renamed and restocked to the note: **Scrapling** (A3 H1 D1), **Sentry** (A5 H1 D3), **Hulk** (A3 H4 D3), plus **Jammer** (A3 H2 D1, +1 Armour to other robots on its tile) and **Grappler** (A4 H2 D1, Snare on its sting), both appearing from Level 2; the spawn roster and per-kind drop chances follow the note (Scrapling 15%, Sentry/Jammer/Grappler 25%, Hulk 35%). **Boss Health is now `base + per-player`** (the note's "X + Y" column) instead of a flat per-hero multiple, and every boss is Armour 5-6, Damage 4, reward 3. Character sheets match the archetype table: the Arrow Squid drops to 3 High dice and 2 loot spots, the Treefrog rises to 5 Low dice, the Comoros Weaver to 2 loot spots. The status die covers the Scrapling, Sentry, Grappler and Jammer per the note's thresholds. **New playable totem: the Wolf** (Striker melee, HP5, 4 High dice): signature Group strength (heal 1 at turn start with an ally on your tile), unlocks Pack Tactics (+1 hit with an ally on your tile) and Frenzy (a free attack die on each kill). Jammer and Grappler enemy sprites ingested; the Scrapling/Hulk/Sentry folders renamed to match. Wolf totem art and a dedicated Sentry sprite are pending (both fall back). New Go tests for the Wolf's three specials, the Jammer aura, the boss-Health formula and the drop chances; the crawler architecture doc and the `make_tiles.py` docs were rewritten to match.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: the hazard and status system (Level 2+).** Hazards and statuses now begin at Level 2 (Level 1 stays clean). **Hazard rooms** seed a hazard on their squares when laid: **live wiring** (1 damage to cross, Electrocuted if you end on it), **coolant flood** (Snares you), **sensor fog** (hides the tile's robots and loot until a hero is on or next to it), **trap** (a mine that bursts once for 3), **overheat** (2 damage and Electrified if you end on it). **Statuses** are small debuffs carried until cleared: **Electrocuted** (-1 to each die), **Snared** (can't leave your tile), **Electrified** (-1 health a round), **Drained** (can't gain Courage), **Rusted** (one fewer die). A robot's hit rolls a **visible status die** (a d6 shown over the board) that lands the status only at or above the kind's per-level threshold, so the Scrapling's sting grows nastier with depth. A spring or the shop washes off every status; the Treefrog's **Spores** clears the Mend target's statuses; **Grounding Boots** make you immune to the electrical ones and the **Torch** clears a tile's hazards. New Level-2 tiles added to the art map, engine kinds and deck (shrine, city gate, and the three Woodland hazard rooms), with ruins/machine rooms reserved for later biomes. Art for the new tiles is pending (they fall back to the styled cell until it lands). 16 new Go tests.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: loot drops on the floor, with a visible drop die.** When a robot dies it rolls a visible **d100** against its per-kind drop chance (shown as a die that pops over the board: roll vs the needed percentage, hit or miss). A dropped object lands **on the monster's square**. If the hero who landed the kill is standing on that tile, they pick it up automatically; otherwise the object stays on the floor and any hero **on that tile** can click it to grab it (no sniping loot from the tile next door). Heroes can also **drop** an object from a character-sheet slot, and it stays exactly where it was dropped. A boss drops 2 consumables + 1 permanent onto its tile. New Go tests cover drop-to-floor, on-tile-only pickup, drop, auto-pickup and the drop roll.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: the shop holds five objects and refills as you buy.** The kangaroo's stall now offers **five** random objects at once (any gear or consumable), and each purchase is immediately **replaced by a new random object**, so the stall never thins out mid-level.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: the remaining character specials are now live.** **Lucky Paws** (Raccoon) gives one **free reroll** each turn (the reroll button shows the free source); **Big Score** (Raccoon) adds a fourth object slot and shakes an extra consumable from your kills; **Bloom** (Treefrog) makes your Mend also heal every other hero on your tile 1; **Ferry** (Weaver) lets your Carry cross a tile edge into the next room; **Flock** (Weaver) makes the Carry a two-square move; generic totems' **Resolve/Second Wind** give a small start-of-turn Shield/heal. Spores still awaits the hazard system. New Go tests per special.
- `[FIX]` **Crawler: the boss cannot be sniped from an adjacent tile.** A ranged attacker (Arrow Squid reach, or a Scope) can still pepper ordinary robots next door, but the boss must be fought from its own tile. Gambler's Glove's free reroll (and now Lucky Paws) is surfaced to the client so the reroll affordance reflects it instead of always charging Courage.
### 0.4.15 — 2026-10-11
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: objects moved off the dice column.** A hero's objects are now **slots on the character sheet** (filled or empty, each with an ⓘ showing what it does), and the slot count is **per totem** (the Beaver carries 2, others 3). Consumables are used from their slot; permanents are passive. The **shop** is now a **callout over the board** (solid panel with its own scroll and readable per-item descriptions) shown while you stand on a shop tile, instead of buttons wedged next to the dice.
- `[FIX]` **Crawler: the healing-spring tile renders again, and special tiles own their shape.** The healing spring is a T-junction; its client art base is back in sync with the engine (`[T,T,T,F]`), so it draws instead of falling back to the plain glyph. Each special tile (portal, healing, shop, vault) now carries its **own edge config** (`crSpecialBase`) rather than borrowing a base path shape, and the Beacon/Sap placers orient the new tile so it connects.
- `[FIX]` **Crawler: boss specials and the draw now follow the design note exactly.** The boss is drawn from the bosses **eligible at the current level** (not just the biome), so Level 1 stays damage-free (Mossback/Vespex/Pylon) and escalates with depth. Specials were reworked to the note's effects: **Adaptive Plating** climbs +1 Armour each round until a landed 6 resets it (was absorb-first-hit); **Hardened Core** is immune until a separate **weak-point** on its tile is destroyed (was a per-round cap); **Overload** applies the new **Electrified** status (1 health/round until a heal; Grounding Boots grant immunity) instead of +2 Damage; **Enrage** is +1 Damage per round survived; **Summoner III** spawns a Sentry + a Scrapling; **Shockwave** hits only heroes on its tile; **Split** breaks off a second weaker core. Tests updated to each.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: the Vault tile.** A one-time loot cache (dead-end nook) that appears from Level 2 (`buildDeck` gates special tiles by level now): stepping onto its centre grants one guaranteed consumable, then the tile is spent (dimmed). Art slot `tile-9`; the per-level tile roster lives in [docs/crawler-architecture.md](docs/crawler-architecture.md). Hazard rooms, shrine and gateway tiles remain unwired (they need the hazard system / are cosmetic). 2 new Go tests.
- `[FEATURE]` **`scripts/make_tiles.py --only N`** extracts a single cell from a grid sheet (e.g. `--rows 3 --cols 3 --only 3 --start 9` grabs the 4th tile as `tile-9`), for dropping in one new tile from a multi-tile sheet.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: objects and the kangaroo shop.** The design note's 16 objects are wired: 8 **permanents** (Spare Die, Whetstone, Lucky Charm, Gambler's Glove, Scope, Plating, Harness, Grounding Boots) as passives, and 8 **consumables** (Medkit, Grenade, Recall Whistle, Battery, Torch, Courage Horn, Beacon, Sap Seed) as one-use actions, each with the note's Courage cost and effect. A hero carries up to **3**, carried across levels (Courage resets). The **shop** (on the shop tile) restocks each level and sells for Courage; defeated robots drop a consumable on their per-kind chance and the boss drops 2 consumables + 1 permanent. 16 icons ingested via a new `scripts/make_objects.py`; the client shows a pack row, use/buy controls and a Lucky-Charm die nudge. Torch and Grounding Boots are ownable but inert until the hazard/status systems exist. 8 new Go tests.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: biomes renamed to the note's names and tiles foldered per biome.** The first biome is **Woodland** (was Forest). Tile art moved to `sprites/crawler/tiles/<biome>/`; the client picks the folder by level (`biomeTileDir`), falling back to woodland until ruins/machine sheets land. Mossback (the Forest boss) now reuses the existing bear sprite.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: multi-level progression with a per-level boss pool.** A run is now **six levels across three biomes** (Forest 1-2, Ruins 3-4, Machine 5-6). Each level is a fresh map (`startLevel`) that draws **one boss at random from its biome's pool**; beating it descends a level (Courage resets, health refills, the party regroups), and beating the Machine boss (The Core) wins the run. Ten named bosses, each with a **signature special** wired into combat: **Adaptive Plating** (absorbs the first hit each round), **Summoner I/II/III** (reinforce with drones), **Jammer Field** (+1 Armour to other robots), **Shockwave** (round-end hit on the whole party), **Hardened Core** (caps damage taken per round), **Overload** (+2 Damage), **Split** (sheds drones at half health), **Enrage** (+2 Damage below half). Backend split into `crawler_bosses.go` (roster + specials); Vespex and Pylon sprites ingested, the rest use the generic boss art for now. HUD shows the level and biome, and a boss shows its name and specials. Stats are a tunable first pass; the roster table is in [docs/crawler-architecture.md](docs/crawler-architecture.md). 10 new Go tests. (The objects/loot economy from the same roadmap cluster is not in this change.)
- `[FEATURE]` **New game: Skyjo (2-8).** `internal/game/skyjo.go` (slug `skyjo`, 7 Go tests): the push-your-luck grid game. Each player has a face-down 3x4 grid from a 150-card deck (-2 x5, -1 x10, 0 x15, 1-12 x10); flip 2 to open, then on your turn draw the deck (keep it over a grid card, or discard it and flip one) or take the top discard (and swap it in). Three equal face-up cards in a column clear to 0. Turning your whole grid up closes the round (everyone else gets one more turn); the closer's score doubles if it is positive and not the lowest; first total past 100 ends the game, lowest wins. Mostly-public board with the active player's drawn card kept private. Client `games/skyjo/SkyjoPage.tsx`, rules card, 3-language title/blurb, route. Deck and closer-penalty confirmed against the published rules.
- `[FIX]` **Bomberman also rejects a mismatched room.** The cross-game join guard now covers Bomberman's own transport (`net.ts`/`GameRoom`), not just the `useRoom` games, so joining a non-Bomberman room's code on the Bomberman page shows the "join that game" notice too.
- `[DOCS]` **Issues & Roadmap tidied.** The implemented games (Codenames, The Mind, The Game, Wavelength, Just One, Love Letter, Decrypto, Hanabi) and other shipped items moved out of **Open** into a condensed **Done**; Open now lists only genuinely-outstanding work (in-game i18n, playtests, Bomberman polish, finder/data items, infra/security).
- `[FIX]` **Games: joining a room created for a different game no longer garbles the UI.** The welcome frame now carries the room's **game slug**; each game page compares it to its own and, on a mismatch, shows "this code is a <Game> room" with a button to **join that game** (the room code carried over), instead of rendering another game's state frames. The slug is treated as untrusted: it is only ever looked up in a fixed client allowlist, never used to build a path, so an unknown slug yields no link.
- `[SECURITY]` **Games: client command frames are size-capped at the trust boundary.** `Room.Command` drops any frame over 8 KiB before it reaches game parsing. Command decoding is also centralised in a shared `decodeCmd[T]` helper (adopted by the crawler), giving one place to harden parsing; decoding stays lenient so valid partial frames still work.
- `[PERF]` **Games: identical per-seat frames are skipped.** A private view byte-identical to the one already sent to a client is not re-sent (the UI renders the latest state, so it carries nothing). Reconnect-safe (a reclaimed seat gets a fresh connection and a full frame via `Joined`); realtime Bomberman is unaffected (it broadcasts). Chosen over a delta protocol, which would add diff state and reconnect edge cases for no benefit at this scale.
- `[REFACTOR]` **Crawler code split for readability.** `crawler.go` (1656 lines) is carved into `crawler_view.go` (the per-seat DTOs + `viewFor`), `crawler_sheets.go` (character sheets + dice) and `crawler_combat.go` (attacks, group fight, shields, healing, rewards), leaving state + turn flow in `crawler.go`. On the client, the pure helpers (tile-art rotation, the walk-sprite direction picker, effective Armour) moved to `games/crawler/logic.ts` with **12 new unit tests** (`logic.test.ts`), the first crawler client coverage.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: a kill's Courage is shared by the whole tile.** Defeating a monster now grants its Courage reward to **every living hero on the monster's tile** (plus the ranged hero who struck it), not just the attacker, so the group that fought it all benefit.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: a healing spring restores full health.** Stepping onto a spring's centre now heals to **max HP** (was +2), still free and repeatable.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: the boss is harder to hit.** Boss Armour is now **5-6** (randomised per run), up from 4, so only strong dice (or the Squid's Barbed Shot) land on it. Note and docs updated.
- `[FIX]` **Hanabi: the final round now includes the player who emptied the deck.** Running the draw pile out is not an instant loss: it starts a final round where each player takes one more turn, then the game is scored on the stack tops. The count was off by one (`len(order)`), so the player who drew the last card was skipped; it is now `len(order)+1`, giving a full round that includes them, per the standard rule ([official rules](https://officialgamerules.org/game-rules/hanabi-rules/), [BoardGameArena](https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/Gamehelphanabi)). The 50-card deck (5 colours x `{1,1,1,2,2,3,3,4,4,5}`) was verified correct. New regression test.
- `[CHORE]` **Changelog moved to [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).** The full dated history lives in its own file now; the README keeps the [Issues & Roadmap](README.md#issues--roadmap) and points here. Keeps the README navigable.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: several robots can share a tile.** `rollMonster` now returns a small group (a lone robot, a pair, or a late scrap swarm of three), and each is laid on its own walkable square (centre first, then the doorways). A 3x3 tile fits up to three. To tune the mix or add a swarm, edit `rollMonster` / `crawlerTilePool` / `buildDeck` in [`crawler.go`](src/internal/game/crawler.go) (all three carry a one-line "this is the place to tweak" note). New test: a swarm spreads over distinct walkable squares and all surface in the view.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: enemies show the Courage they grant.** The inline badge is now `🛡armour ❤health 💥damage ✊reward`, so it is clear each defeat earns Courage and how much (drone 1, brute and tank 2, boss 3), matching the enemy table in the design note.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: the boss Health scales with the party (~3 per hero).** Was a flat 10, which a big party melts and a solo hero cannot dent at the right pace. Now `3 x players` (minimum 3), so the fight runs about the same few rounds at any count. Aligned to the design note's scaling prose. New test across 1/2/4/6 players.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: a defeated robot stays on its tile.** It keeps its square in its **destroyed** sprite frame (dimmed, no bob, no stat badge) instead of vanishing, so you can see what you cleared.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: tidier character sheet.** Dropped the useless "Attack: any" threshold (Attack takes any die; the Armour decides the hit, still explained in its ⓘ) and the "low/balanced/high dice" label from the sheet. The die type still drives the rolls; it just no longer clutters the UI.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: you can no longer attack with a die that cannot land.** An Attack die below the target's (effective) Armour is now refused outright instead of being spent on a guaranteed miss: low dice go to Move or your Special, which is the point of the die map. (Supersedes the 0.4.13 "any die may attack, it just misses" rule.)
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: the Arrow Squid's specials are now live.** **Barbed Shot** (active at 3 Courage) lowers a target's Armour by 1 for the Squid, so a lower die lands; **Deep Volley** (active at 5 Courage) makes an attacking 6 count as two hits, once a turn. Its ranged attack and Jet dash were already in. (The Squid's **Jet** dash is its designed signature special, so only the Squid dashes; it is not a universal move.)
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: a 2-square move is now directed step by step.** A fast mover's Move die and the Squid's Jet no longer force a straight line: you take the first step, then direct the remaining step(s) separately (so left-then-up works). Carried on the wire as `bonusMoves` with a "Keep moving (N left)" control; the turn will not auto-end while steps remain.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: portal, healing-spring and shop tiles.** The start tile is now a **transporter**; a second portal is shuffled into the deck, and standing on a portal's centre lets you teleport to another portal you choose (free, repositioning). Two **healing springs** and one **shop** (a merchant stall, inert until the loot system) are shuffled into the bands. Art maps to tile-5/6/7 from the new woodland sheet.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: the Arrow Squid no longer dashes.** Its Jet dash is removed: the Squid's signature is **ranged attack**, so it has no active special-action slot (its menu is Move / Attack / Courage, plus the Barbed Shot and Deep Volley Courage unlocks). Fast two-square movement stays the Comoros Weaver's thing. (Note and architecture doc updated to match.)
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: the healing spring heals on entry, free and repeatable.** Stepping onto a spring's centre square heals 2 with **no die cost**, and it is **not consumed**: step off and back on to heal again (capped at your max). Was: spend a die, heals once, then depletes. (Note updated.)
- `[FIX]` **`scripts/make_tiles.py --start N`.** The splitter can now number the first cell `tile-N` and **merges** the manifest, so a one-tile sheet overwrites a specific tile (e.g. a corrected T-junction over `tile-3`) without renumbering or dropping the others. (The documented "overwrite a given tile" did not work before, because every run started at `tile-0`.)
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: heroes walk and face like in Bomberman.** The board pawns now use the totem atlas's walk frames instead of a single standing pose: moving left or right plays the **side** walk (mirrored for left), moving up the **back** walk, moving down the **front** walk, then the pawn rests facing where it last went. A fallen hero shows the **defeated** frame, and on a loss (party wipe or the collapse countdown running out) the whole party drops to that frame. Reuses the same atlas and frame layout Bomberman draws from (`useHeroSprites` diffs each hero's cell between server states to pick the pose and run the cycle). Closes the 0.4.6 "use the walk/orientation sprites in the crawler" follow-up.

### 0.4.14 — 2026-10-11
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: group fights, damage at end of the round.** Monsters no longer strike after each hero's turn. Once every hero has acted, each surviving monster hits **every living hero on its tile** (Damage minus that hero's Shields). Allies pool their blows across the round (persistent Health), and the Beaver's **Bulwark** (when active) soaks an ally's hit.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: specials auto-activate by Courage.** The manual unlock button is gone. A special is active whenever Courage is at or above its threshold (3, then 5) and switches off again if Courage drops below it, with a power-on pulse. Courage is a live power level, not a purchase.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: Courage action blocked at the cap (10)**; it frees up again as soon as Courage is spent (e.g. a reroll).
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: more character specials wired.** Arrow Squid's **Jet** (dash 2) and **ranged** attack (hit an adjacent tile), Comoros Weaver's **Carry** (give an ally a 1-square move) and **fast move** (2 squares per Move die). Raccoon's Pilfer still awaits the loot system.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: enemy stats on the board, and an F3 debug overlay.** Each enemy shows `🛡armour ❤health 💥damage` inline. Tile labels and the monster hover box moved into an F3 overlay (like Bomberman). A fallen hero now shows its **defeated** sprite frame; a monster that just struck plays its **attack** animation.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: Courage reward scales with enemy toughness.** Drone (Scrapling) 1, brute and tank (Hulk) 2, boss 3. (The boss ends the level on defeat, so its reward rarely matters.)
- `[FIX]` **Crawler: ability tooltips are solid.** The real cause was an ancestor `opacity` on dimmed specials making the hover box translucent; inactive specials are now dimmed by colour instead.

### 0.4.13 — 2026-10-11
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: Attack is tied to the enemy, not a die threshold.** Any die may now be spent as an attack die; it lands a hit only if it meets the target monster's Armour (a miss otherwise). The menu shows "Attack: any" and each attack button shows ✓hit / ✗miss for the selected die. (Was: a fixed Attack >=4 gate.)
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: weighted dice per character.** Totems roll one of three die types from the design, not a plain d6: Low `{1,2,2,3,3,6}`, Balanced `{1,2,3,4,5,6}`, High `{2,4,5,5,6,6}`. A High die meets Armour far more often, so the die type is the striker-vs-guardian lever.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: the five designed characters.** Beaver (Guardian, 4 Low, HP7), Arrow Squid (Striker, 4 High, HP4), Treefrog (Healer, 4 Low, HP6), Raccoon (Trickster, 5 Balanced, HP5), Comoros Weaver (Carrier, 5 Low, HP5), each with its own health, dice, menu and two named specials; other totems use a generic sheet. Wired specials: Brace (Beaver) and **Mend** (Treefrog, new heal). The Squid/Raccoon/Weaver loot/carry/reposition specials are V1 placeholders (Guard), noted in-tooltip.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: tank and level-1 boss sprites.** Ingested `Adversary-Tank.jpeg` (Hulk, 3/4/3) and `Adversary-BossLevel1.png` via `make_enemies.py`; the boss now draws its sprite instead of 💀, and the tank spawns in the mid/late bands. Boss stat line aligned to the design (Armour 4, Health 10, Damage 4).
- `[FIX]` **Crawler: the Courage bar shows the real cap (10), not 5.** The bar fills toward 10 with tick marks at the two unlock thresholds (3 and 5).

### 0.4.12 — 2026-10-11
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: running the tile stack out is no longer an instant loss.** When the deck empties it starts a **5-turn collapse countdown** (every drawn tile has been laid by then, so the boss lair is already on the map): reach and defeat the boss within those turns or the dungeon collapses. The lay phase is skipped while the stack is empty, and the HUD shows a pulsing "collapse in N turns" warning.
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: no dice left auto-ends your turn.** Spending your last die ends the turn automatically, so you no longer have to click End turn with nothing to do.
- `[CHANGE]` **The Mind: a mistake now restarts the level.** Playing out of order still costs a life, but instead of discarding the lower cards and limping on, the whole level **re-deals and restarts from the top** (house rule). The HUD flashes the breaking heart and a "the level restarts" banner.
- `[FEATURE]` **Hanabi: host can restart at any time.** The "New game" button already showed on game over; the host now also has a confirmed **↻ Restart** in the HUD during play, to recover a stuck or spoiled table.
- `[FIX]` **Crawler: ability info tooltips are readable.** The hover box (e.g. Bulwark's passive) is now fully opaque with a high stacking order, and its panel lifts above the dice column while open, so nothing bleeds through it.

### 0.4.11 — 2026-10-10
- `[BUG]` **Crawler: the LAY phase could not place a tile (sometimes not at all).** 0.4.10 placed the proactive tile at a single server-chosen cell **next to your own tile** and let you only rotate, so a dead-end would not connect and a hero standing on a fully surrounded tile was **skipped entirely** ("unable to lay tiles at all"). The LAY phase now offers **every legal placement across the whole frontier**: you pick the cell **and** the rotation (drag onto any glowing cell) and lay the tile where the paths line up. A hero only skips when nothing fits anywhere on the board. The move-triggered lay in the spend phase stays rotation-only (its cell is fixed by where you stepped). (New tests: connected placements across the frontier, and a boxed-in hero still lays.)

### 0.4.10 — 2026-10-10
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: a round now lays, then plays.** Both tile-laying mechanics are kept together. Every round opens with a **LAY phase**: each hero, in turn, draws one tile that the server places at a frontier next to them (a mandatory, proactive lay), and the hero picks only its **rotation**. Then the **SPEND phase** runs the dice turns as before, and stepping onto a frontier mid-turn still lays a tile there (the move-triggered lay). In both cases the player chooses the rotation only, never the location. After the last hero spends, a new round opens at the lay phase again.
- `[DOCS]` Rewrote the crawler turn model as a two-phase round (new state diagram, lay-phase prose, the `phaseLay` wire flag, a note on faster stack drain and the `buildDeck` knobs).

### 0.4.9 — 2026-10-10
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: tiles are laid by moving again.** Reverted the separate lay round: stepping out of your tile onto a frontier draws a tile and lays it **right there**, and you choose only its **rotation** (not its location) before the move completes onto it. This is the organic explore-as-you-go loop, kept together with the rotate-and-drop UX. (Tests rewritten for move-triggered laying.)
- `[CHANGE]` **Crawler: Beaver shields no longer stack.** Shields are now a **per-turn** resource: whatever you had or gained this turn (Thick Hide's +2, Brace, Guard) is cleared at the end of your turn, so they can't pile up across turns. Thick Hide simply re-grants its 2 at the start of your next turn. (New test.)
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: enemy attack animation.** When you are fighting a monster (it is engaged this turn) it shows its **attack** frame and does a little lunge, instead of only the idle pose.
- `[FEATURE]` **Crawler: second enemy type (brute).** A tougher bipedal robot, split with [`scripts/make_enemies.py`](scripts/make_enemies.py) into `sprites/crawler/enemies/brute/`. Monsters now carry a `kind` on the wire, and the client maps it to the right sprite (weak **drone** vs tougher **brute**; boss still 💀). The brute shows up on the mid/late bands.
