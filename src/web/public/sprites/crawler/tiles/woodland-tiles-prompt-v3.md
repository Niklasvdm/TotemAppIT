# Woodland tileset prompt, V3

Reference prompt for generating the woodland Level-2 tiles (new tiles only).
One sheet, all tiles together, path reaches the centre of each open edge so they connect.

---

One tileset sheet for a top-down fantasy board game: all the tiles below arranged together
on a single sheet as separate square tiles, even spacing, no frame present at all.

Each tile is a 3x3 grid of floor squares seen from directly above, a deep, dark, infested
WOODLAND: denser canopy, long shadows, broken robot debris and sparking wires creeping
among the roots. Painted storybook fantasy style, consistent scale and lighting. Blocked
(closed) edges are dense bushes, rocks or roots.

PATH RULE: every tile MUST show a bare dirt path that reaches the exact centre of each of
its OPEN edges and connects across the tile, so tiles snap together edge to edge. The
feature or hazard sits ON or BESIDE that path, it never replaces it. A closed edge has NO
path and is solid forest or wall right to the tile border. The path must read clearly as
the walkable route.

CRITICAL, the image must NOT contain any of these: no gridlines or grid overlay of any kind,
no border or frame around the tiles or around the whole sheet, no background scenery behind
the tiles (the space between and around tiles is plain solid white and empty), no text, no
numbers, no icons, no UI, no characters, no drop shadows. The tiles sit as separate cut-outs
on empty white.

The tiles:
1. Vault (dead end, 1 open edge): the dirt path enters from one edge and leads to a cache at
   the centre, a hollow mossy log or a half-buried wooden chest spilling gears, crystals and
   salvaged trinkets beside the path.
2. Live-wiring room (straight, 2 open edges on opposite sides): the dirt path runs straight
   across between the two opposite edges; broken robot cabling and exposed wires arc with
   electric sparks over the middle of that path.
3. Coolant-flood room (T-junction, 3 open edges): the dirt path branches to three edges; a
   flooded coolant pool and boggy mud spread across where the routes meet, broken pipes, a
   reflective sheen, reeds and wet moss.
4. Sensor-fog room (crossroads, 4 open edges): the dirt path crosses as a four-way junction
   reaching all four edges; thick drifting mist hangs over it, dense ferns, half-hidden
   broken robot parts.
5. City gate (1 open edge, a one-way exit): the dirt path enters from the forest edge only
   and leads to a crumbling stone gateway in an ivy-covered city wall opposite; the woodland
   stops dead at the stone, ruined overgrown streets glimpsed through the arch, the way into
   the ruins.

Square image, high detail, crisp readable shapes, top-down orthographic view. Reminder:
plain empty white background, no gridlines, no frame, no border.
